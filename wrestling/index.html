Before you sign up

Tournaments listed on USA Wrestling need a current USA Wrestling card, and you can buy one at usawmembership.com. OKWA tournaments run under the Oklahoma Kids Wrestling Association, so check okwa.us for their card and rules.

Sign up sites like USA Bracketing, FloArena and Trackwrestling ask you to make a free account the first time. Weights, brackets and times can change after sign ups close, so check the event page the night before.
