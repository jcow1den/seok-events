Basketball: Season Opening

Oklahoma splits its tip-off. Small schools are already playing, and the bigger schools, including McAlester, open in December.

  • Season opener windowMcAlester is 5A

    Classes 2A-6A tip off statewide

    Tue Dec 1

    Oklahoma's 2A through 6A basketball season starts Dec 1. McAlester plays in 5A, so the Buffaloes and Lady Buffaloes open from this date. Classes A and B started Nov 2 and are already in their season.

Baseball, Softball & Spring Sports

Off-season right now. This panel is ready for spring 2027 schedules, scores, and tournaments.

Baseball, fastpitch and slowpitch softball, soccer, track, and golf return in the spring. When a local school or travel team posts a spring schedule or a tournament in Southeast Oklahoma, it will be listed here the same way wrestling is listed, with dates, location, and a link to register or follow along.
